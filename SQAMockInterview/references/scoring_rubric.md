# SQA Mock Interview Scoring Rubric

## Score Scale (1-5)

### 5 — Excellent: Interview-Ready
**What this looks like:**
- Deep technical understanding and domain knowledge
- Considers edge cases and trade-offs
- Mentions specific tools, frameworks, or technologies
- Shows strategic thinking (not just tactical)
- Addresses team/collaboration aspects
- Explains reasoning clearly
- Acknowledges constraints or unknowns appropriately
- Well-structured, articulate answer

**Feedback:** "This is a strong answer. You'd likely advance to the next round."

**What would make it even better:** [Minor gaps, if any]

---

### 4 — Strong: Ready with Minor Gaps
**What this looks like:**
- Solid technical understanding
- Covers main points but misses some nuance
- Mentions some tools/frameworks
- Good structure but could be more strategic
- May miss edge cases or team aspects
- Mostly clear reasoning

**Feedback:** "Good answer with solid fundamentals. You're on the right track."

**What would strengthen it:** [Specific gaps to address]

---

### 3 — Adequate: Needs Development
**What this looks like:**
- Correct fundamentals but lacks depth
- Misses industry context or best practices
- Vague on specifics (no tools/frameworks mentioned)
- Doesn't address edge cases
- May miss team/communication aspects
- Partially addresses the question

**Feedback:** "You understand the basics, but you'd benefit from going deeper."

**What to work on:** [Areas needing more knowledge/thought]

---

### 2 — Developing: Significant Gaps
**What this looks like:**
- Some correct ideas but many gaps
- Missing key concepts
- Doesn't mention relevant tools or approaches
- Incomplete thinking
- Misses major considerations
- Doesn't fully address the scenario

**Feedback:** "You're on the right path, but there's a lot to develop here."

**Key gaps:** [What's missing]

---

### 1 — Needs Work: Fundamentals Unclear
**What this looks like:**
- Incorrect or incomplete understanding
- Doesn't address the scenario adequately
- Missing fundamental concepts
- No mention of relevant tools or approaches
- Unclear or confusing reasoning
- Would not advance in a real interview

**Feedback:** "This needs more work. Let's focus on the fundamentals."

**Areas to study:** [Core concepts to revisit]

---

## Evaluation Dimensions

When scoring, consider these dimensions:

### 1. Technical Knowledge (35%)
- **Correct understanding** of concepts (automation, CI/CD, APIs, etc.)
- **Depth** — goes beyond surface-level
- **Current practices** — aware of modern tools and approaches

**Strong answer includes:**
- Specific tool names (Selenium, Jest, GitHub Actions, Postman, JMeter, etc.)
- Framework/architecture knowledge (POM, microservices testing, etc.)
- Understanding of trade-offs

---

### 2. Problem-Solving & Strategy (35%)
- **Structured thinking** — breaks down the problem logically
- **Strategic approach** — not just "here's what I'd do" but "here's why"
- **Edge cases** — considers what could go wrong
- **Measurability** — mentions how success would be measured

**Strong answer includes:**
- Clear problem decomposition
- Consideration of alternatives and trade-offs
- Metrics or success criteria
- Risk awareness

---

### 3. Communication & Collaboration (20%)
- **Clarity** — explains ideas clearly
- **Team awareness** — mentions collaboration, stakeholders
- **Practical reality** — acknowledges constraints
- **Articulation** — well-structured response

**Strong answer includes:**
- Clear examples or analogies
- Recognition of different stakeholder needs
- Acknowledgment of limitations ("I'd need more info about...")
- Structured reasoning

---

### 4. Industry Best Practices (10%)
- **Awareness** of standards and patterns
- **Modern approaches** (shift-left, DevOps practices, etc.)
- **Compliance thinking** when relevant (GDPR, security, etc.)

**Strong answer includes:**
- References to industry standards (OWASP, ISTQB concepts, etc.)
- Knowledge of current trends
- Compliance awareness when relevant

---

## Domain-Specific Scoring Notes

### Test Automation
**Look for:**
- Framework knowledge (Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, etc.)
- Understanding of test design patterns (Page Object Model, etc.)
- Flaky test debugging experience
- Test maintenance strategies

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses frame architecture and maintainability
- Mentions specific design patterns
- Considers scalability

---

### CI/CD
**Look for:**
- Pipeline stage understanding (build, test, deploy)
- Tool knowledge (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, etc.)
- Environment parity awareness
- Deployment strategies (blue-green, canary, etc.)

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses advanced strategies (progressive rollouts, feature flags)
- Mentions monitoring and observability
- Addresses failure scenarios

---

### API Testing
**Look for:**
- HTTP understanding (methods, status codes)
- Test organization and frameworks (REST Assured, pytest, etc.)
- Contract testing awareness
- Performance baseline understanding

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses distributed system challenges
- Mentions API versioning and backward compatibility
- Addresses eventual consistency concerns

---

### QA Strategy
**Look for:**
- Risk-based testing approach
- Coverage decision-making
- Shift-left understanding
- Metrics and measurement awareness

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses team dynamics and change management
- Shows business acumen
- Mentions cross-functional collaboration

---

### Test Data Management
**Look for:**
- Data generation approaches (factories, fixtures, API-driven)
- Privacy and compliance awareness (GDPR, CCPA)
- Test isolation strategies
- Cleanup and state management

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses synthetic data generation
- Addresses performance optimization
- Shows security awareness

---

### Performance Testing
**Look for:**
- Load vs. stress vs. spike testing understanding
- Tools (JMeter, Locust, k6, etc.)
- Metric understanding (latency percentiles, throughput)
- Bottleneck identification strategies

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses capacity planning
- Mentions profiling and optimization
- Addresses chaos testing or resilience

---

### Security Testing
**Look for:**
- OWASP top 10 awareness
- Basic vulnerability testing (SQL injection, XSS, CSRF)
- Tool knowledge (OWASP ZAP, Burp Suite)
- Compliance thinking when relevant

**Higher scores when:**
- Discusses SAST/DAST/SCA scanning
- Mentions secret scanning and supply chain security
- Addresses compliance requirements

---

### Test Leadership
**Look for:**
- Team communication skills
- Root cause analysis thinking
- Coaching and mentoring awareness
- Process improvement mindset

**Higher scores when:**
- Shows emotional intelligence
- Discusses change management
- Mentions metrics and data-driven decisions

---

```
- [Specific strength 2]

**Where you can improve:**
- [Gap 1]: [Why this matters]
- [Gap 2]: [How to address it]

**Industry best practice:**
[What's typically expected at your difficulty level]

**Next time, consider:**
[Specific actionable suggestion]
```

## Comparative Feedback (End of Interview)

After all questions, synthesize:

**Overall Score:** Average of all question scores

**Strengths (themes):**
- What patterns showed up in your strong answers?
- What did you do consistently well?

**Development Areas (themes):**
- What gaps appeared across multiple answers?
- What skills need more development?

**Interview Readiness:**
- For beginner: "You're building strong fundamentals. Keep practicing X."
- For intermediate: "You're ready for most interviews. Focus on Y to stand out."
- For advanced: "You're interview-ready. To go deeper, work on Z."

**Next Steps:**
- [Specific study recommendation]
- [Specific practice recommendation]
- [Follow-up question area]
